anonymous_namespace{Log.cpp}::LeakedState Struct Referencefinal

Description

Owns all process-lifetime logging state.

The singleton is intentionally allocated once and never destroyed. This avoids static-destruction ordering problems and keeps queues, caches, handles, and emergency buffers available to fatal and crash handlers until the process exits.
