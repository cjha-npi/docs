npi::ind::SlotGate::Blocker Class Referencefinal

#include <SlotGate.hpp>

Description

Scoped object that keeps its associated SlotGate blocked.

A Blocker increments the associated gate's blocking count during construction and decrements it during destruction.

Blocker objects cannot be copied. They can be move-constructed so they can be stored in std::optional and returned from tryEnter_(). A moved-from Blocker becomes inactive and does not modify the count when destroyed.
